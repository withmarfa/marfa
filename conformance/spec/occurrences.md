# Occurrences and recurring events

An occurrence is one time an event happens. A recurring event is one `core.event` item, a series, carrying RFC 5545 rule lines in `recurrence`; its occurrences are computed when a window is read and never stored. `GET /occurrences?from&to` answers them (`search-and-filters.md` 21). This chapter states how a series unfolds, what a window includes, and what a write may store.

## How a series unfolds

1. A series' first occurrence is its own `starts_at`, whether or not its rule would produce that time, and its rule's occurrences follow it, as RFC 5545 counts a series: `COUNT` includes the start. Each computed occurrence carries `series_id`, and lasts as long as the series' first occurrence does: `ends_at` less `starts_at`, else `duration` in seconds, else no length, when an occurrence carries no `ends_at`. `compliance/recurrence.test.ts › counts its own start as the first occurrence and carries its length to each`, `› takes its length from duration when it states no end`.
2. A series with a `timezone` keeps its local hour in that zone across a clock change, so its occurrences sit at a different UTC hour on either side of one; a series with none advances in UTC. `compliance/recurrence.test.ts › keeps its local hour across a clock change in its zone`.
3. **A rule value that names an instant applies at that instant.** An `UNTIL` in UTC ends the series at that instant in every zone, east or west of UTC, and an `EXDATE` or `RDATE` in UTC, or carrying a `TZID` naming any zone, removes or adds the occurrence at the instant it names. A value with no zone is read in the series' own zone, and a date-only `EXDATE` removes the occurrence on that day there. `compliance/recurrence.test.ts › ends a series at a UTC UNTIL's instant in %s`, `› applies an EXDATE and an RDATE at their instants, in UTC or another zone`.
4. **A local time a clock change skips or repeats resolves one way in every zone**, as RFC 5545 reads a date-time: a skipped time is read with the offset in force before the gap, so it lands later by the length of the gap, and a repeated time takes its first reading. A recurring occurrence at such a time is kept, not dropped. `compliance/recurrence.test.ts › moves a skipped time later by the gap in %s`, `› takes the first of a repeated time in %s`.
5. **A whole-day series (`all_day: true`) occupies whole days in its zone.** Each occurrence starts at local midnight and ends at local midnight as many days later as the first occurrence spans, so a day a clock change shortens or lengthens is still one day. Its rule repeats by days or longer; an hourly, minutely or secondly rule, or one naming hours, minutes or seconds, is refused (8). `compliance/recurrence.test.ts › spans whole local days across a clock change`.
6. **A stored exception replaces the occurrence it names.** An event with `original_starts_at`, joined to its series by a `parent-of` edge from the series, takes the place of the occurrence at that instant, carrying `series_id` and `replaces`, and is shown at its own times. `compliance/recurrence.test.ts › shows a moved occurrence only in the window its own times overlap`.
7. **Unfolding is bounded inside each step.** Each candidate time a rule considers is counted, and a series whose rule considers more than its bound, or takes longer than its bound, before its walk through the window ends is stopped where it is, named in `series_errors` and counted in `scan.series_unexpanded`, and the answer carries `expansion_incomplete: true`, while the rest of the window answers. `compliance/recurrence.test.ts › is stopped inside its walk and named, and the rest of the window answers`.

## What a write may store

8. **An event's schedule is judged on every door that writes an item's properties**: `POST /items`, `PATCH /items/{id}`, `POST /items/bulk`, `POST /items/bulk-actions` with `update_properties`, and `POST /admin/restore-archive`, each refusing as it refuses any other property (`items.md` 24). Refused `400 invalid_properties`, with `details.errors` naming the field: a `recurrence` line that cannot be read or would not be applied as written (an `EXRULE`, an `RDATE` period, a part RFC 5545 does not define for the rule's frequency); a rule that names no date that exists after the start, such as the 30th of February; a rule that produces no occurrence after the start within the bound a read unfolds a rule to (7); a rule with no `starts_at` to unfold from; a `timezone` or `end_timezone` that the zone database does not resolve as a named zone; a `duration` below 0 or above a century; a second `RRULE`; a `recurrence` over 40,000 characters in all, refused before any line is read; and more than 1,000 added and removed dates together. A rule that ends by its own `COUNT` or `UNTIL`, a fixed-offset zone such as `Etc/GMT+5`, and a rule naming a day only some years have, such as the 29th of February, are taken. `compliance/recurrence.test.ts › refuses %s, naming the field`, `› refuses the same rule added by an update or a bulk write, and stores none of it`, `› refuses it on a bulk-action patch per row, and in a restored archive`, `› takes an ordinary series, a fixed-offset zone and a leap day`.

## What a window includes

9. **A window includes every event that overlaps it**, as RFC 4791 reads a time range: one that starts before the window ends and ends after it opens, so an event or a series occurrence already running when the window opens is included, and one ending exactly as it opens is not. An event with no length is included where it starts, `from` inclusive and `to` exclusive. An event's end is its `ends_at`, else its start plus `duration`, else, for a whole-day event, the day after its start. `compliance/recurrence.test.ts › includes every event that overlaps it, and no event that ends as it opens`.
10. **A moved occurrence belongs to the window its own times overlap**: a window holding only the slot it left shows nothing there, and a window its own times overlap shows it once. `compliance/recurrence.test.ts › shows a moved occurrence only in the window its own times overlap`.
11. A window longer than 400 days is refused `400 validation_error` with `details.max_days`, and one holding more than 5000 occurrences is refused `400 validation_error` with `details.max_occurrences`, rather than either being trimmed. `compliance/recurrence.test.ts › refuses a window longer than 400 days, and one holding more than 5000 occurrences`.
